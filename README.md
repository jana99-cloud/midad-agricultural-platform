# 🌱 NAMA'A | Smart Agricultural Monitoring Platform

**Smart Agricultural Monitoring & Field Transformation using NASA Data in Jazan, Saudi Arabia**.  
*Submitted to: NASA Space Apps Challenge*

---

## 🎯 Overview & Problem Statement
Farms in Jazan face growing climate pressures, while farmers need earlier warnings to prevent unexpected losses. **NAMA'A** addresses key regional challenges:
* **Rising Heat:** Extreme temperatures strain crops and reduce productivity.
* **Scarce Water:** Limited water availability makes irrigation increasingly challenging.
* **Shifting Vegetation Health:** Changes in plant health signal deeper ecosystem stress.
* **Unpredictable Rainfall:** Erratic rains disrupt planning and increase risk for farmers.

---

## 💡 The Solution: NAMA'A
NAMA'A bridges the gap between satellite data and field decisions by providing smart monitoring, early risk detection, and actionable agricultural recommendations:
* **Vegetation Health Tracking:** Monitors crop conditions from `Excellent` to `Very Poor`.
* **Early Risk Identification:** Detects elevated heat and water stress before crop damage occurs.
* **Actionable Guidance:** Suggests optimized irrigation scheduling and monitoring for vulnerable fields.

---

## 🛰️ NASA Data Sources & Tech Stack
NAMA'A integrates multi-source Earth observation data and machine learning:
* **Landsat 8 & 9 (OLI / OLI-2):** 30m resolution for Vegetation Index calculation ($NDVI = \frac{NIR - Red}{NIR + Red}$).
* **MODIS MOD13Q1:** 250m resolution (16-day observations) for seasonal vegetation tracking.
